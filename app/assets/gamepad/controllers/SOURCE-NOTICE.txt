# ExoSnap 2D controller artwork

All 2D controller layouts were substantially reworked and extended by ExoSnap
contributors. Their work includes redrawn wireframe contours, control artwork,
consistent line weights, separate moving controls, pressure and knockout
surfaces, clipping and additional controller variants. This includes the
redesigned Steam Controller and Steam Deck layouts.

These revisions are credited to ExoSnap contributors. Original artwork sources
and their license notices remain credited below.

## Steam Controller 2026 generic fallback

The steam_controller_2026 layout contains exact copies of generic/source.svg
and its exported element geometry. The Xelu/Luna body and shoulders retain the
CC0-1.0 dedication, and the AL2009man controls retain the MIT notice, credited
in the Generic controller section above. The SPDX expression is
MIT AND CC0-1.0. Export root IDs identify this layout without changing geometry.

This is generic artwork, not an exact Steam Controller 2026 silhouette or a
claim of qualified hardware support. Its mapping retains the saved layout ID,
standard controls and independent stick clicks. Quick Access remains inert,
including the generic Share symbol. No paddle or touch-surface graphics are
supplied. No Steam Controller 2026 model-derived geometry is included in the
editable source or element exports.

Exact-model artwork requires documented sufficient rights or reviewed
independent replacement provenance before inclusion. These notices describe
the supplied replacement materials, not a legal clearance of the application.
